Privacy
Policy.

This policy explains how the SMS 2 GS Android application accesses, uses, and protects data from your device and Google account.

The app requests sensitive SMS permissions and uses Google API Services to interact with Google Sheets. This page explains what that means for your data.

01

Data we collect

The app reads incoming SMS messages that match user-defined rules. This may include the message text, sender phone number, timestamp, and any other SMS content required to satisfy the user’s configured rules.

The app does not read or process SMS messages that do not match the user-defined rules.

02

How we use the data

The data is transmitted solely to the user’s personal Google Spreadsheet via Google Sheets API. No data is sent to our own servers or to any other third-party servers.

The purpose of this data transfer is to help the user store and organize specific SMS messages in their own Google account for personal use, reporting, or record-keeping.

03

Third-party services and disclosure

The app uses Google API Services to interact with Google Sheets. Data handling by Google’s services is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share SMS contents or spreadsheet data with any third parties other than Google services used to provide the app’s functionality.

04

Data storage and retention

SMS data processed by the app is stored only in the user’s own Google Spreadsheet and remains under the control of the user’s Google account.

The app does not maintain its own external database; all data retention is governed by the user’s settings and Google’s policies for their account and Google Sheets.

05

Security

Access to Google Sheets is authorized using Google’s secure OAuth mechanisms, and the app only uses the scopes required to write SMS data to the user’s spreadsheet.

Users are responsible for securing their device, SMS inbox, and Google account, including enabling features such as two-factor authentication, to protect access to their data.

06

Children’s privacy

SMS 2 GS is intended for general use by adults and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect or process SMS data for the purpose of profiling or targeting children.

07

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to comply with changes in Google Play policies, Google API Services User Data Policy, or applicable privacy regulations.

When we make material changes, we will update this Privacy Policy page and, where appropriate, update in-app notices so users can review the new terms before continuing to use the app.

08

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how SMS 2 GS handles your data, please contact the developer at piuroymani@gmail.com.
